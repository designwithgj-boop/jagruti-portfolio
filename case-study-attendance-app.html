01 / The Challenge

Field Workers Faced Frustrating Check-In Delays & Spotty Connectivity

Delta Infosoft’s client organizations employed thousands of field technicians, logistics drivers, and on-site retail staff across Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities in India. The legacy attendance process required workers to either sign physical registers or navigate cumbersome, desktop-ported web portals on small phone screens.

Key pain points uncovered during user research:

• Congestion at Shift Handover: Workers stood in long queues at branch biometric devices, causing recurring check-in delays.
• Weak Network Dropouts: When cellular connectivity dropped inside warehouse basements or transit routes, the existing app silently failed to log punch-ins.
• Lack of Leave Visibility: Employees had no quick way to check their remaining paid time off or shift changes without calling HR.

02 / UX Strategy & Thumb-Zone Design

Designing for Single-Handed Operation in Variable Lighting

Field employees routinely use their devices while walking, wearing protective gloves, or balancing equipment. Through contextual inquiry with 18 field workers, I established core mobile design guidelines:

Legacy Experience

  • Small 28px tap targets causing frequent miss-clicks
  • Multi-step modal dialogs requiring both hands to enter dates
  • No visual confirmation of whether GPS geofence was active
  • Complete crash when offline; loss of unsubmitted check-in

Redesigned Experience

  • 56px minimum touch targets optimized for the natural thumb arc
  • 1-tap swipe confirmation with haptic feedback vibration
  • Live green pulse badge verifying geofence boundary in real-time
  • Local SQLite queue caching timestamps and auto-syncing upon reconnect

04 / Design System & Guidelines

Cross-Platform Cohesion Across Android & iOS

To support both native iOS (Human Interface Guidelines) and Android (Material You / Material 3), I created a unified token library in Figma:

• Adaptive Components: Bottom navigation sheets dynamically adjust border radii and interaction physics based on the target OS.
• High-Contrast Color Palette: Tested under direct midday sunlight (WCAG AAA rating) to guarantee screen legibility outdoors.
• Clear Error Recovery: If GPS signal is lost, intuitive inline recovery cards prompt the user to enable Wi-Fi assistance without blocking check-in queues.

05 / Outcomes & Takeaways

Empowering 12,000+ Field Workers Daily

The redesigned Attendance App launched successfully across client organizations in manufacturing, retail, and field logistics. Within 8 weeks, daily on-time check-in compliance climbed to 94%, manual HR correction requests dropped by 65%, and store managers reported eliminating morning shift queues altogether.
