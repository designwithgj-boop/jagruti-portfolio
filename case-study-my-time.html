01 / The Challenge

High Cognitive Overhead in Legacy Scheduling

Delta Infosoft's My Time product is an enterprise SaaS workforce management platform utilized by manufacturing and corporate clients to manage thousands of employees. Over several years of iterative feature bloat, the scheduling and attendance module had become fragmented.

Department leads and operations managers were forced to navigate a disjointed 5-step modal workflow just to assign weekly shifts or adjust leave approvals. Heavy data density led to frequent human errors, overlooked overtime limits, and frustrated administrative staff.

Before (Legacy Workflow)
  • 5-step modal wizard with high drop-off rates.
  • No visual conflict detection when double-booking staff.
  • Inconsistent table columns and fragmented action menus.
  • Absence requests hidden 3 clicks deep in sub-settings.
After (Redesigned Experience)
  • 2-step inline guided schedule matrix with instant save.
  • Visual conflict badge & smart rule validator.
  • Unified design system components across all 8 modules.
  • 1-click approval sidebar for leaves and swap requests.
02 / Research & User Discovery

Understanding the Dual Needs of Managers & Employees

I conducted stakeholder interviews and contextual inquiries with 12 operations supervisors and 8 HR administrators. The research uncovered two primary insights:

1. Scanning Speed Over Deep Drills: Managers spend less than 3 seconds per screen evaluating shift statuses. They required high visual hierarchy—clear colored badges and scannable matrices—rather than dense text-heavy rows.

2. Immediate Reversible Actions: Accidental shift assignments caused anxiety. The interface needed clear undo states, inline validation, and non-blocking notifications.

04 / Design System

Standardizing Component Tokens for Engineering Handover

To prevent design regression across Delta Infosoft's agile development sprints, I built a comprehensive Figma UI kit containing:

• Atomic Tokens: 8-point spatial grid, accessible contrast color pairs, and strict type scales.
• Modular Data Cells: Interchangeable table components with states for hover, error, empty, and loading skeleton.
• Interactive Prototypes: High-fidelity micro-interaction flows for shift drag-and-drop and leave approvals, tested with developers before code commit.

05 / Results & Learnings

Measurable Impact Across Client Deployments

Following rollout across enterprise client pilots, the redesign achieved a 38% reduction in weekly scheduling duration and received unanimous approval from operations leads. Front-end engineers reported a 50% faster feature velocity when building subsequent reporting pages because of the standardized design tokens.
