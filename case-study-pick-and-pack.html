01 / The Challenge

High Velocity Fulfilment Crippled by Misaligned Mobile & Desktop Systems

Large retail distributors using Delta Infosoft’s supply chain software faced escalating returns and shipping delays. Warehouse pickers carried handheld scanner devices with small, low-contrast text interfaces that were hard to read in dim warehouse aisles. Simultaneously, warehouse supervisors at the central dispatch desk used a separate desktop dashboard with mismatched batch IDs and status names.

Key operational bottlenecks identified:

• Disoriented Route Navigation: Pickers zig-zagged aimlessly across aisles due to unstructured item lists.
• Barcode Verification Frustration: Weak feedback on scanned items resulted in double scans or skipped inventory.
• Blind Spots for Supervisors: Dispatch managers couldn’t see real-time picker bottlenecks until the shift deadline passed.

02 / Dual-Sided Strategy

Harmonizing the Mobile Picker & Web Supervisor Journeys

I conducted on-site contextual walkthroughs at 3 fulfillment facilities, observing pickers wearing gloves under varying warehouse lighting. The core solution required synchronizing two distinct user personas with a shared design token system:

Legacy Workflow

  • Flat item tables with tiny font sizes requiring pinch-to-zoom
  • Silent scan errors requiring workers to check text readouts
  • Supervisors manually refreshed desktop browsers for updates
  • Desynchronized status tags between mobile and desktop

Redesigned Experience

  • Guided linear aisle cards highlighting the immediate target bin
  • High-contrast green/red audio-visual haptic scan confirmation
  • Live WebSocket updates feeding manager dispatch queues in real-time
  • Unified nomenclature and semantic tokens across both platforms

04 / Design Tokens

Cross-Platform Component Library & Dark-Zone Tokens

Because pickers move between brightly lit packing tables and dim high-bay storage racks, I architected a specialized high-contrast design token system:

• High-Contrast Typography: Strict 600+ font weight thresholds and tabular numerals ensuring serial codes are effortlessly readable at arm's length.
• Audio-Visual Synchronization: Distinct chime tones mapped to specific color pulses (success chirp with green edge-glow; warning double-tone with amber indicator).
• Shared Component Architecture: Single source of truth in Figma exported directly to developer tokens for both React web and Android Jetpack Compose views.

05 / Results & Takeaways

45% Reduction in Errors & Rapid Operational Adoption

Following pilot deployments across three tier-1 logistics hubs, error logs verified a 45% reduction in wrong-bin misplacements. Average order pick cycles fell from 14 minutes down to under 6 minutes, accelerating daily truck dispatches and earning high praise from warehouse operations management.
